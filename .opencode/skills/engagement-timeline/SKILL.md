# Engagement Timeline

Load this skill with `backend`, `frontend`, or `qa` when the task touches activities, tasks, or the shared record timeline.

## Module map

- Spec: `openspec/specs/engagement-timeline/spec.md`
- Routes:
  - `src/app/(app)/tasks/**`
  - Timeline sections are embedded in company, contact, deal, ticket, and campaign detail pages.
- Components: `src/components/timeline/**`, `src/components/tasks/**`
- Server Actions: `src/actions/engagement.ts`
- Services: `src/lib/services/timeline.ts`, `src/lib/services/tasks.ts`
- Validation: `src/lib/validation/engagement.ts`
- Schema: `activities`, `tasks`
- Tests: `tests/integration/engagement.test.ts`, `tests/e2e/02-directory.spec.ts`, `tests/e2e/03-deals.spec.ts`

## Invariants

1. An activity must link to at least one company, contact, deal, ticket, or campaign. A task must also link to at least one record.
2. Activity types are `note`, `call`, `email`, `meeting`, and `stage_change`. `stage_change` entries are generated by the sales module; do not create them manually.
3. An activity's occurrence time cannot be in the future. Authors can edit their own activities; administrators can delete activities.
4. The record timeline is newest-first, filters by type, paginates older entries, and includes completed tasks.
5. Task due dates cannot be in the past when creating or editing. Only active users can be assigned.
6. Completing a task records the actor and timestamp. Completed tasks are separate from open task views, can be reopened, reassigned, edited, or deleted.
7. Task views group my tasks and expose filters; a task appears on its linked record and in the global task list.
8. Activities and tasks are shared across the workspace. Do not add per-user visibility rules beyond author-edit/admin-delete for activities.
9. `src/lib/services/timeline.ts` also resolves link labels for record pages; keep it free of UI imports.

## Workflow

1. Read the engagement-timeline spec and the affected service.
2. Keep link validation and authorization in services/actions, not components.
3. Add integration coverage for link rules, edit/delete permissions, and task lifecycle.
4. Verify the timeline section on at least one record page and the task views in E2E.
